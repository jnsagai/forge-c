# Safety Analysis / ISO 26262 Software Evidence

ASIL allocation and safety plan reference; HSI and technical safety requirements; failure modes, diagnostic mechanism assumptions, failure cause/effect, detection/timing response, safe-state/FTTI, partition/concurrency interference, residual risk/deviations and evidence references.

Use FMEA/DFA (and other analyses) where justified by safety plan. Safety manager decides scope and additional required methods. This template is not a complete ISO 26262 work-product checklist.
